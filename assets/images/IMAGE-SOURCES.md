The site shipped with more placeholder slots than there were real photographs. Rather than leave placeholders visible, these were removed from the pages:

- `weddings/wedding-21.jpg` (one figure in The Portraits chapter)
- `newborn/newborn-07, -11, -12, -14.jpg` (the whole "Family Moments" chapter on newborn.html)
- `maternity/maternity-04, -06, -07, -08, -09, -10, -11, -12.jpg` (one gallery figure, the second story image and the whole "Portraits" chapter on maternity.html)
- `toddlers/toddler-04.jpg` (one figure in the Toddlers gallery)

To bring any of them back, add a photo with that filename and copy a `<figure class="gallery-item …">` block from the same page.
