A quiet introduction

We photograph the moments people forget to notice. A hand held a little tighter. The pause before a vow. The first quiet weeks at home.

Two bodies of work, one way of seeing. Weddings photographed like films, with light and honesty. And little memories, newborns, mothers-to-be and small children, photographed gently in the time they are given.

About the studio

Photographs made to be looked at for years, not days.

Memory Portraits By Nandhu is a photography studio in Kollampalayam, Tamil Nadu. Wedding stories are shared under the studio name, and newborn, maternity and childhood work under Little Memory Portraits. Same eye, same care.

Our photographs are quiet, honest and made with care. The kind you print, frame and pass down.
