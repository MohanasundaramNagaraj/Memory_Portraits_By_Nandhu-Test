Light, and the waiting

A portrait of a moment you are still inside.

Maternity photographs are strange and wonderful. You are photographing someone who is not quite here yet, and a version of yourself that will soon be gone.

We keep them quiet and honest. Simple clothing, soft light, real expressions. No props, no clichés, nothing you will wince at in ten years.

  • Studio
  • Outdoors

How a session feels

Slow, warm and led by you.

We usually photograph between the 30th and 36th week, when there is a beautiful shape and still enough energy for a gentle afternoon. Wear what feels like you. Bring your partner, your children, your dog.

Most of the session is simply talking, moving a little and letting the light do its work.

You will not remember how tired you were. You will remember that someone thought this was worth photographing.
