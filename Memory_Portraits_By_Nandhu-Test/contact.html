After you write to us

What happens next.

  1. i

    We reply, personally

    A message back from the studio, usually the same day, to check the date is free and hear a little about you.

  2. ii

    A short conversation

    On a call or over coffee at the studio. Where, who, what matters most. We listen more than we talk.

  3. iii

    The day itself

    We arrive early, stay quiet and stay close. You will forget we are there, which is the point.

  4. iv

    Choosing and finishing

    Every photograph is selected and finished by hand. No batch presets, no rush.

  5. v

    Yours to keep

    A private online gallery to share with family, and albums designed page by page when you would like one.
